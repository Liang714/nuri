The world, Nuri's signature

Each food group you eat today becomes a place on your world. Groups from earlier in the week rest as outlines; nothing ever shrinks or disappears, and there is no count to beat. Logging a food makes its place glow once (a crossfade when Reduce Motion is on). VoiceOver reads the world as a sentence, never as a score.

  • Vegetables, #4F8A5B
  • Fruit, #D07A9A
  • Grains, #E2B54E
  • Protein, #7E4127
  • Legumes and nuts, #8B7AC0
  • Dairy and alternatives

Food-group colors appear only in the world and its legend. Sources, ads and notices never reuse them. Each place has a darker ring and a white gap so neighbors stay distinct with color blindness; with Differentiate Without Color on, places also get textures.

Rules engineers build to

When numbers are hidden

  • Hides energy, nutrients, grams, activity minutes, weekly totals and exercise energy on every screen, including Diary and search.
  • Portions use household measures; food detail shows its food group instead of a nutrition grid.
  • The plan shows days, not minutes. No ads appear.
  • The setting lives in You, Display, and is named Hide numbers, never by condition.

Large text

  • From accessibility sizes, label and value rows stack vertically and the logotype hides.
  • The world shrinks beside its sentence; buttons become full-width rows.
  • Tab labels hide and icons grow; the week strip scrolls sideways.
  • Korean product names wrap to two lines before truncating. Numbers and units never split.
